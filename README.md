## 💡 Strategic Insights & Key Findings

- **The 144-Roster Bottleneck**: With only 12 franchises and 12 roster spots (144 total), WNBA draft picks face the highest roster friction in professional sports.
- **UConn Dominance**: Connecticut produced five #1 overall picks (Sue Bird, Diana Taurasi, Tina Charles, Maya Moore, Breanna Stewart).
- **Round 1 Longevity**: First-round picks average 8.4 years in the league compared to under 2 years for Round 3 draftees.
- **International Pipeline**: International European prospects drafted in Round 1 achieve high impact after draft-and-stash commitments.
